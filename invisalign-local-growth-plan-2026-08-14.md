# Invisalign Local Growth Plan

**Practice:** Uplift Dental & Orthodontics, Garden Grove, California  
**Prepared:** August 14, 2026  
**Objective:** Expand qualified Invisalign consultation discovery and conversion around the current central Garden Grove cluster without unsupported claims, review manipulation, or attribution confusion.

## Executive Decision

**Invisalign is the strongest measurable near-term local opportunity.** The Local Falcon baseline places Uplift at rank 1 at the business center, with 13 of 49 points in the top 20 and a 6.12% top-three share. This is materially stronger than the broad `dentist near me` and `braces` scans, and it provides a defensible starting wedge for local growth.[1]

The current `/invisalign` page already has the right conversion structure: a clear free-consultation proposition, the confirmed **Platinum Invisalign Provider** designation, board-certified orthodontic guidance, call and text actions, FAQ content, local relevance, digital-scanning detail, and privacy-safe tracking. Its organic visibility is not yet proportional to that page quality because the currently live host still serves generic fallback HTML for non-root routes. The staged Phase 2 release solves that raw-response problem and must precede any aggressive organic expansion.[2] [3]

> **Primary strategy:** Protect Uplift’s center-point Invisalign visibility first, increase qualified consultation conversion, then expand the nearby rank-3 and rank-8-to-18 cells. Do not chase the full five-mile grid with thin location pages, new GBP categories, or unsupported claims.

Google states that local ranking is driven mainly by **relevance, distance, and prominence**. Complete business information improves relevance; distance cannot be overridden directly; and prominence includes signals such as links and reviews.[4]

The staged package already has route-specific initial documents, self-canonicals, initial JSON-LD, correct coordinates, a registry-generated sitemap, true redirects, and real 404/410 behavior. Do not create Los Alamitos, Seal Beach, or Leisure World pages before this release is live and verified.

## Priority 2 — Build Prominence Without Manipulating Reviews

The appropriate review operation is a neutral, optional invitation following eligible completed visits. Google allows a business to ask for honest feedback using a link or QR code, but prohibits incentives for reviews, review changes, or removal of negative reviews.[5]

> **Approved neutral wording:** “Thank you for choosing Uplift Dental & Orthodontics. If you would like, you can share honest feedback about your experience on Google: [review link]. Your feedback helps future patients learn about the practice.”

Do not ask a patient to mention Invisalign, request a star rating, selectively invite only likely-positive reviewers, or offer an orthodontic discount in return for feedback. The operational target is **consistent availability of the invitation**, a timely privacy-safe response workflow, and authentic profile activity—not a review-count quota.

## Priority 4 — Content Only After the Technical Release Is Verified

The goal is not more generic Invisalign pages. After the production technical release is verified, publish only a small decision-support cluster that answers real consultation questions and strengthens `/invisalign` through internal links.

| Proposed asset | Purpose | Non-negotiable content guardrails |
|---|---|---|
| “Invisalign or braces: how an orthodontic consultation helps compare options” | Supports comparison intent and links back to the consultation page. | No claim that either option is universally better, faster, or suitable for every case. |
| “What to bring to an Invisalign consultation” | Reduces scheduling friction. | Use only verified office policies; no patient-data collection or medical intake form. |
| “How digital scans are used in orthodontic planning” | Builds on the existing Trios 6/iTero factual section. | Explain that records and recommendations vary; do not promise treatment outcomes. |

Do not launch the deferred city pages until the technical release and the 30-day comparison are complete. A location page is not a substitute for relevance, distance, or prominence.
