# Uplift Dental — 30-Day Local Prominence Operations Plan

**Purpose:** Convert the August 14, 2026 Local Falcon baseline into a disciplined, policy-safe operating rhythm. This plan is designed to expand authentic local proof and improve discovery without manipulating reviews, collecting PHI, or making unsupported claims.

> Google allows businesses to ask customers to use a review link or QR code. It prohibits incentives in exchange for a review, review changes, or removal of a negative review.[1]

## Operating Principles

| Principle | Required behavior | Prohibited behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Honest feedback | Invite eligible completed-visit patients to share honest feedback. | Asking for a five-star rating, screening by expected sentiment, or sending unhappy patients to a different channel. |
| Privacy | Use neutral wording and no clinical details. | Mentioning a diagnosis, treatment, appointment, or confirming that someone is a patient in public. |
| Consistency | Make the same optional invitation process available across eligible completed visits. | Staff review quotas, selective outreach, purchased reviews, or family/staff reviews. |
| Accuracy | Use verified Uplift services, hours, providers, locations, and assets. | Keyword stuffing, name changes, fake claims, or copied competitor content. |

## Daily and Weekly Workflow

| Cadence | Owner | Action | Non-PHI measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| After eligible completed visit | Front desk | Offer the neutral Google QR card or approved review link. | Daily aggregate number of invitations offered; do not store names in the log. |
| Twice weekly | GBP owner | Check new reviews and flag any response requiring owner approval. | Number of new reviews and response backlog. |
| Within two business days | Approved reply owner | Post a short, privacy-safe review reply when appropriate. | Response time; do not track reviewer health information. |
| Weekly | GBP owner | Publish one factual profile update only if there is a real, approved message or asset. | Post date and source record reference. |
| Weekly | Marketing owner | Check NAP consistency and progress on approved citation applications. | Submission/verification status only. |
| Day 30 | Marketing + owner | Re-run matched Local Falcon grids and compare against the baseline. | SoLV, top-20 coverage, ARP, ATRP, and business-action trends. |

## Front-Desk Review Invitation

### Printed card / QR copy

> **Thank you for choosing Uplift Dental & Orthodontics.**  
> If you would like, please share honest feedback about your experience on Google. Your feedback helps future patients learn about the practice.  
> **[Scan QR code / Visit review link]**

### Verbal prompt

> “If you would like, we have a Google review card here. Honest feedback helps other people learn about the practice.”

The invitation must be optional. Staff should not discuss a desired star rating, ask a patient to mention a particular service, or follow up differently based on the patient’s expected opinion.

## Privacy-Safe Review Reply Templates

| Situation | Template |
|---|---|
| Positive feedback | “Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback. We appreciate your kind words and look forward to welcoming you again.” |
| General concern | “Thank you for sharing this. We are sorry your experience did not meet expectations. Please contact our office directly at (714) 898-3308 so we can listen and help.” |
| Detailed clinical review | “Thank you for your feedback. To protect privacy, we cannot discuss details here. Please contact our office at (714) 898-3308 so our team can assist.” |

Each reply should be reviewed for privacy and factual accuracy. Google recommends professional, relevant, concise responses and warns against disclosing a reviewer’s private information.[1]
