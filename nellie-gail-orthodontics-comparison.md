Uplift should **not** replicate unverified rating counts, patient outcomes, static testimonial text, generic “top” claims, insurance-coverage promises, or keyword-stuffed city lists. Uplift’s improvement path is factual topical coverage, helpful FAQs, clear conversion paths, physician/technology provenance, and restrained internal linking.
