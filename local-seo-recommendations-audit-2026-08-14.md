## Why the Remaining Suggestions Are Not Website Tasks

Google explains that local results are primarily influenced by relevance, distance, and prominence. Complete factual information, review replies, and authentic photos can help, but a site page cannot directly override distance or purchase a ranking advantage.[1] The current scan’s near-field pattern is therefore not evidence that additional city-name pages would be effective.

Reviews and other Maps content must be based on genuine experiences. The resulting policy-safe approach is a consistent optional review invitation and general, privacy-safe replies—not review coaching, incentives, or selective invitations.[2] [3]
