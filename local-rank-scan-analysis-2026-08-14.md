## 30-Day Review and Local Prominence Plan

### Days 1–3 — Lock the measurement and invitation process

Run the two exact `best dentist near me` scans using the same provider. Retain the exports, settings, time, and map pins. Do not substitute a signed-in personal search for either scan.

Create one neutral Google review link and QR code. Use the same optional invitation after each eligible completed visit, through a front-desk card or approved post-visit message. Google allows a business to ask customers using a review link or QR code, but it prohibits incentives offered in exchange for reviews or review changes.[6]

> **Approved neutral wording:** “Thank you for choosing Uplift Dental & Orthodontics. If you would like, you can share honest feedback about your experience on Google: [review link]. Your feedback helps future patients learn about the practice.”

Do not ask for a specific star rating, filter invitations by expected sentiment, offer a discount, or include diagnosis/treatment details in the invitation. Track only an **aggregate daily count** of invitations made—not patient names or clinical details.

### Days 4–10 — Convert existing profile strength into credibility

Reply to the two pending Google reviews with approved privacy-safe language. Aim for review monitoring twice each week and response drafting within two business days. Never confirm that a reviewer is a patient or discuss any treatment, appointment, or health information.

Complete a factual GBP service check against current provider availability. Keep `Dentist` as the primary category. Update only true service gaps, and keep the orthodontic/Invisalign specialty structure accurate. Publish one factual GBP update using an approved office/community asset; use it to explain a verified service or booking path, not a guarantee, discount, or manufactured testimonial.

### Days 11–20 — Strengthen the local entity footprint

Pursue the three documented accurate-entity opportunities—Zocdoc, Garden Grove Chamber, and City of Garden Grove BigG—only where Uplift is eligible and can maintain the record. Use the canonical business name, address, main phone, website, actual hours, and CareStack booking destination. These are consistency and prominence projects, not paid rank shortcuts.

For Invisalign, use the existing verified practice strengths on factual pages and GBP services: Garden Grove location, free consultation if currently offered, board-certified orthodontist, Platinum Invisalign provider, all-age consultations, and the exact scheduling route. Do not add pricing, guaranteed outcomes, or unsupported insurance assertions.

### Days 21–30 — Re-measure and direct effort

Repeat all four existing scans with identical settings, then compare top-20 coverage, SoLV, ARP, and ATRP. Compare the exact-query scans separately. Pair the maps with GBP Performance: generic discovery terms, calls, direction requests, website clicks, and booking clicks.
